# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Camilo Oviedo for Technical Business Analyst, Business Systems Analyst, and Application Analyst roles. Secondary audiences are engineering leaders and collaborators who need evidence that Camilo can translate operational needs into reliable technical delivery.

## Product Purpose

This portfolio helps a hiring decision-maker quickly understand Camilo's professional value, inspect his complete personal, open-source and professional project portfolio, review his career history, and contact him or download his current resume. Success means a recruiter can identify role fit and credible evidence without reading a long online CV.

## Positioning

Camilo works at the intersection of business operations and software delivery: analysing requirements and workflows, communicating across technical and non-technical stakeholders, and contributing to the implementation and improvement of operational systems. His experience spans financial systems, workflow automation, software modernisation, and technical documentation.

## Operating Context

The portfolio is evaluated quickly, often on mobile or desktop during recruiting. It presents professional work in banking, fintech, IT consulting, operations automation, and open-source software. Visitors can move between the home page and existing project-detail routes, follow valid external project and professional links, contact Camilo by email or LinkedIn, and download his resume from Cloudflare R2 through the existing `RESUME_PDF_URL` environment variable.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve Astro, Tailwind CSS, minimal JavaScript, the existing routes, and the Vercel deployment adapter.
- Preserve Cloudflare R2 resume delivery through `RESUME_PDF_URL`.
- Preserve valid external links and factual content already present in the repository.
- Prioritise Technical Business Analysis, Business Systems Analysis, Application Analysis, requirements and process analysis, stakeholder communication, systems automation, software delivery, and financial-domain experience.
- Camilo has confirmed 8+ years of professional experience; keep experience dates and source-backed achievements as the authority.
- Do not invent metrics, technologies, employers, clients, projects, education, certifications, testimonials, or outcomes.
- Keep the implementation lightweight, semantic, responsive, keyboard accessible, high-contrast, and respectful of `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Brand Commitments

Use the name Camilo Oviedo and a professional, precise, concise, human voice. The portfolio should position Camilo primarily for analyst roles with strong technical delivery experience, not as a generic developer. Existing portrait photography and project screenshots may be used when they materially support the narrative.

## Evidence on Hand

- Dated roles and achievement statements in `data/experience.json`.
- Project descriptions, technologies, routes, screenshots, and external links in `data/projects.json` and `public/`.
- Portrait photography in `src/img/profile_image.webp` and `src/img/casual_image.webp`.
- Contact details and resume delivery references in the existing components and `README.md`.
- No education or certification records are present in the repository; future work must not fabricate them.

## Product Principles

1. Lead with role fit and professional value before chronology.
2. Preserve the complete project record across Personal, Open Source Contributions and Professional Projects while keeping each entry concise and ordered.
3. Make contribution, context, technology, and supported outcomes easy to scan.
4. Preserve factual credibility by distinguishing repository evidence from open decisions.
5. Keep contact and resume actions obvious without turning the portfolio into a sales landing page.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA practices, including semantic landmarks and headings, keyboard operability, visible focus, sufficient contrast, descriptive alternatives for meaningful images, comfortable target sizes, responsive reflow, and reduced-motion support.
